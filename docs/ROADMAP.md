# Roadmap

## 90-Day Validation Plan

The roadmap is intentionally narrow. The objective is to produce evidence for the business, not to build a full property management platform in the first release.

## Month 1: Discovery And Foundations

### Deliverables

- Internal stakeholder interviews
- Incident taxonomy
- Initial SLA policy
- Minimum data model
- Build versus integrate decision
- Clickable prototype
- Operational workflow map

### Outcome

Validated problem, clear MVP scope, and enough workflow detail to avoid automating a broken process.

## Months 2-3: Incident MVP

### Deliverables

- Internal incident inbox
- Ticket creation
- Email and app intake concept
- WhatsApp intake concept
- Ticket identity and linking
- SLA visibility
- Status model
- Assignment model
- Response workflow
- Close and reopen flow
- AI triage assistance

### Outcome

Centralised and traceable incident operation in a controlled pilot.

## Months 4-5: Rent Operations

### Deliverables

- Rent calendar
- Payment due dates
- Payment status tracking
- Late payment alerts
- Initial reconciliation workflow
- Internal follow-up queue

### Outcome

Rents become visible and operationally managed instead of handled through fragmented manual follow-up.

## Month 6 And Beyond: Reporting

### Deliverables

- Monthly report template
- Income and expense summary
- Rent status
- Relevant incident history
- Internal reporting view
- Investor-facing reporting view

### Outcome

Investor performance becomes explainable and trustworthy because reporting is based on cleaner operational data.

## Sequencing Logic

1. Incidents first because they are frequent, visible, and operationally bounded.
2. Rent operations second because they carry direct financial impact and need stronger process/data maturity.
3. Reporting third because automated reporting without reliable underlying data only automates uncertainty.
