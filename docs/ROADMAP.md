# Roadmap

## Product Goal

Build an operational command centre that helps PropHero manage property incidents with higher speed, clearer ownership, and better investor and tenant trust.

## MVP

The first production-ready version should focus on a narrow but valuable workflow:

- central incident inbox
- manual ticket creation and intake
- email and app-originated incidents
- property, requester, and contract association
- SLA priority model
- assignment and status workflow
- internal timeline and audit history
- AI-assisted summary, classification, and next action
- basic reporting on open tickets, overdue tickets, resolution time, and reopened tickets

Success should be measured by operational impact, not feature volume.

## Validation Metrics

Recommended MVP metrics:

- time to first response
- time to resolution
- percentage of tickets assigned within SLA
- percentage of P1 and P2 incidents escalated correctly
- reopen rate
- manual touches per incident
- tickets handled per operations team member
- investor or tenant satisfaction after incident closure

## Phase 1: Operational Control

Objective: replace fragmented incident handling with one controlled workflow.

Key capabilities:

- omnichannel intake into a unified queue
- property and contract matching
- ownership assignment
- SLA timers
- status workflow
- internal notes and timeline
- customer-visible updates

## Phase 2: AI-Assisted Triage

Objective: reduce manual review effort and improve consistency.

Key capabilities:

- automatic category suggestion
- priority recommendation
- summary generation
- missing information detection
- suggested reply drafts
- escalation recommendation
- confidence thresholds and human review

## Phase 3: Automation And Integrations

Objective: connect incident management to the operational ecosystem.

Key capabilities:

- provider routing
- CRM integration
- email and WhatsApp ingestion
- calendar and appointment coordination
- document and image attachment handling
- owner and tenant notification rules
- analytics dashboards for portfolio operations

## Phase 4: Predictive Operations

Objective: use incident data to prevent recurring issues and improve asset performance.

Key capabilities:

- recurring issue detection
- property-level operational health score
- provider performance tracking
- cost and resolution benchmarking
- predictive maintenance signals
- portfolio-level risk reporting

## Key Product Risks

- AI recommendations must remain explainable and auditable.
- Incident ownership must be clear, otherwise queues become another shared inbox.
- Integrations should be prioritised by operational value, not by technical availability.
- The first release should avoid becoming a full CRM replacement.
- Customer-facing communication must be controlled to protect trust.

## Recommended Next Step

Run the prototype with real operations stakeholders and validate:

- whether the queue model reflects how teams actually prioritise work
- which incident categories create the most operational pain
- which integrations would remove the most manual work
- where AI support saves time without increasing risk
