# Measurement Plan

## Objective

Validate whether the incident command centre improves operational control, response quality, and scalability in property management.

## North Star

Managed properties under Property Management.

This metric should grow without proportional growth in operations headcount or deterioration in service quality.

## Input Metrics

### Operational Leverage

- Managed properties per operations team member
- Open tickets per operations team member
- Tickets resolved per operations team member
- Manual touches per ticket

### Service Quality

- Time to first response by priority
- SLA compliance by priority
- Average time to resolution
- Ticket reopen rate
- Percentage of tickets with clear owner
- Percentage of tickets with next action

### Data Quality

- Percentage of tickets linked to requester
- Percentage of tickets linked to property
- Percentage of tickets linked to contract
- Percentage of tickets with category
- Percentage of tickets with priority
- Percentage of tickets with complete closure reason

### Investor Trust Proxy

- Number of investor follow-ups asking for status
- Number of complaints related to lack of visibility
- Investor satisfaction after incident closure
- Repeat incident rate by property

### AI Assistance

- Percentage of AI suggestions accepted
- Percentage of AI suggestions corrected
- Accuracy by category
- Accuracy by priority
- Missing-data detection usefulness
- Analyst time saved per ticket

## Pilot Design

### Baseline

Measure two to four weeks of current incident handling:

- intake channels
- volume by category
- response time
- resolution time
- SLA misses
- manual follow-ups
- reopened issues
- escalations

### Pilot

Run the MVP with a limited internal team and a controlled property subset.

Pilot scope:

- one market or region
- one analyst group
- selected incident categories
- limited investor-facing visibility

### Comparison

Compare baseline versus pilot:

- time to first response
- SLA misses
- ticket traceability
- owner clarity
- manual coordination load
- investor follow-up volume

## Decision Criteria

Continue and expand if:

- SLA visibility improves
- ownership is clearer
- manual follow-up decreases
- data completeness improves
- analysts trust the workflow
- AI suggestions reduce triage work without creating quality risk

Pause or redesign if:

- teams duplicate work in old tools
- ticket data is incomplete
- status changes are unreliable
- AI recommendations create rework
- investor-facing visibility increases support volume
