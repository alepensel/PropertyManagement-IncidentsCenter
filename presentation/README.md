## Core Narrative

PropHero does not only sell access to real estate investment opportunities. It sells the ability to delegate property ownership with confidence.

The risk appears after acquisition, when property management becomes operationally complex: incidents, rent collection, tenants, providers, communication, reporting, and investor expectations.

The recommended approach is to build a focused incident core first. Incident management is the best first wedge because it is visible, frequent, operationally bounded, and creates reusable data for future rent operations, reporting, and AI automation.
